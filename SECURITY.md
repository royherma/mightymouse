# Security

Please report security problems privately. Do not open a public issue.

Email **hello@getmightymouse.com** with the subject "Security" and include:

- what you found and where (file, screen or endpoint)
- steps to reproduce
- what an attacker could do with it

We aim to reply within 3 business days and will tell you when it is fixed.

## In scope

- The Mac app, the phone remote server and its pairing and connection flow
- The iPhone and Android apps
- The native helpers in `native/`
- The `mightymouse-code` connector

Examples: a way to control a computer without pairing, leaking transcripts, screen contents or tokens, running code through a crafted agent message, or escaping the app's permission prompts.

## Out of scope

- Problems that need an already-compromised computer or phone
- Social engineering, spam and denial of service by volume
- Issues in third-party tools we only call (report those to the tool's authors)
- Findings in our hosted services and websites: send those to the same address, they are handled separately

Please give us a reasonable time to fix before you publish details.
